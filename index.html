One step at a time

A clear path to getting started.

You don’t need to have every answer. We’ll walk through the process together.

  1. 01

    Contact Us

    Complete our enrollment form to tell us about your child and family.

  2. 02

    Insurance Verification

    Our team reviews coverage and helps determine available ABA benefits.

  3. 03

    Initial Assessment

    A Board Certified Behavior Analyst assesses your child’s strengths, needs, and goals.

  4. 04

    Individualized Treatment Plan

    We develop a plan informed by the assessment and your family’s priorities.

  5. 05

    Begin Services

    Once approvals are complete and services are available, therapy can begin.
