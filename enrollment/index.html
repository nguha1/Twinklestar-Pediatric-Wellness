A little clarity for the journey

Questions, answered.

What is ABA therapy?

Applied Behavior Analysis uses behavioral principles and data to understand behavior and teach meaningful skills. Care is tailored to each child and family.

Does my child need an autism diagnosis to receive services?

Documentation requirements may vary by insurance plan, clinical needs, and service type. Our team can explain what is needed; eligibility policies are still being finalized.

Do you accept insurance?

The in-network provider list will be added once confirmed. Please contact our team to verify current participation and your plan’s ABA benefits. Coverage is not guaranteed.

Where do you provide ABA services?

We serve North DFW through home-, community-, and school-based services when appropriate. Availability depends on your ZIP code, clinical needs, staffing, and permissions.

How do I get started?

Start with the enrollment form. Once secure intake is available, our team will review your request and discuss coverage, documentation, assessment, and next steps.

How long does the enrollment process take?

This may vary based on your child’s needs, insurance authorization, and service availability. Contact our team for more information.
