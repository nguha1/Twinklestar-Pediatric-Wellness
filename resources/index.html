ABA & Autism Resources

Clear, approachable information to help your family understand ABA and explore the next steps.

What Is Autism Spectrum Disorder?

Autism is a developmental condition that can influence communication, social interaction, sensory experiences, and patterns of behavior or interests. Autistic children have individual strengths and different support needs. Respectful care starts with understanding the whole child.

What Is ABA Therapy?

Applied Behavior Analysis uses behavioral principles and data to understand behavior and teach meaningful skills. Goals are individualized, and progress is reviewed to help guide decisions about care.

Potential Benefits of ABA Therapy

ABA therapy may help support communication, social skills, daily living skills, independence, learning, and coping skills. Treatment may also address behaviors that interfere with safety, learning, or daily life. Needs and outcomes vary for each child.

What Does ABA Therapy Look Like?

Sessions vary depending on the child. They may include play-based learning, natural environment teaching, structured teaching when appropriate, reinforcement, and data collection. Family collaboration helps connect skills to everyday life.

Parent & Caregiver Involvement

Families bring valuable knowledge about their child’s interests, routines, and needs. Caregiver collaboration can help identify meaningful goals and support the use of strategies across everyday situations.

Understanding the ABA Enrollment Process

The process may include reviewing diagnosis documentation, checking insurance benefits, completing an assessment, developing a treatment plan, and obtaining authorization. Requirements and timing vary by plan and availability.
