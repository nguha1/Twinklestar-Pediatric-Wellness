Registered Behavior Technician (RBT)

Registered Behavior Technicians work directly with children under the supervision of a Board Certified Behavior Analyst to implement individualized ABA treatment plans, collect behavioral data, support skill development, and collaborate with the clinical team.

Responsibilities

  • Implement ABA treatment programs
  • Work directly with pediatric clients
  • Collect accurate treatment data
  • Follow behavior support plans
  • Promote communication and independence
  • Participate in BCBA supervision
  • Maintain professional and ethical standards
  • Communicate appropriately with caregivers and clinical staff

Qualifications

  • High school diploma or equivalent
  • Ability to work with children
  • Dependable transportation where required
  • Strong communication skills
  • Ability to follow clinical protocols
