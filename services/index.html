01 / In familiar surroundings

In-Home ABA Therapy

Home is where many everyday moments happen. In-home ABA creates opportunities to practice meaningful skills within familiar spaces and routines, with caregiver participation woven into care.

Treatment priorities are chosen with your family and your child’s needs in mind. Each child’s plan is different.

Possible areas of focus

  • Communication
  • Daily living skills
  • Play skills
  • Social interaction
  • Independence
  • Behavioral support
  • Following routines
  • Caregiver participation

02 / In everyday places

Community-Based ABA

Practice can extend into the places your family visits. Community-based support helps children use skills across settings, with goals centered on communication, independence, and participation.

Natural settings may include

  • Stores
  • Parks
  • Restaurants
  • Community activities
  • Recreational settings

Locations and activities are selected based on clinical recommendations, family priorities, and appropriate permissions.

03 / Alongside learning

School-Based ABA

When clinically appropriate and permitted by the school, ABA services may support a child in their educational environment.

Collaboration matters

Care may involve coordination with educators and caregivers to support individualized goals and consistency across settings. Services are subject to school approval and are not guaranteed at every school.

04 / Building a foundation

Early Intervention

For young children, care can create opportunities to learn through play, connection, and daily routines. Goals focus on skills that are meaningful to your child and family during the early developmental years.

05 / Learning together

Parent & Caregiver Training

Caregiver collaboration helps families understand strategies used in ABA and support consistency outside therapy sessions. We work together around routines and goals that matter to your family.

Topics may include

  • Understanding behavior
  • Reinforcement
  • Communication strategies
  • Daily routines
  • Skill generalization
  • Supporting independence
